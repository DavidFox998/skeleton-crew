## The second law of the house (David, verbatim sense)

The house has two laws now:

1. Nothing leaves this house unmeasured.
2. **What cannot be measured does not leave at all. The house keeps it.**

Whoever is summoned to the mortuary might still be there — if the results come
back unmeasurable, they stay. The back room is where the kept are: the real
dead whose cases never fully measured (Hughes, no will; Poe, claims that do
not measure). "You are not the first" is a statement of occupancy.

## The conversation form (David's direction)

Silas has CONVERSATIONS with the desks. The conversation is asymmetric:

- Silas speaks like a man — pleading, bargaining, joking, lying, grieving.
  His lines carry the emotion; he has issues with the desks.
- The desks answer like procedure — flat, named verdicts, no self, no mercy
  and no cruelty. The Apathy doctrine stands for their register.
- The drama is a man arguing with a wall that is always right.

## The arc (David's shape: climax, anticlimax, letdown)

Mixed outcomes — some passes, some failures. Not uniform refusal.

1. **THE REGISTER (Beacon) — a pass, and a hook.**
   Silas states who he is. The family record measures: Silas Vane, brother —
   PASS. He is admitted. But he says one thing too many ("Elias sent for
   me" / "he wanted me to come") and the desk answers: that claim is not in
   any sealed record; it cannot be measured. The second law is stated to
   him, plainly, as intake information. He laughs it off. The audience
   doesn't.

2. **THE ESTATE (Corwin) — a failure, a pass, THE CLIMAX.**
   He slides the 2019 will: REFUSED · will_superseded. He argues — the desk
   does not. The 2024 distribution then measures, item by item: the bull,
   the telescope, the bell — PASS after PASS, receipts issuing; hope rises.
   Last item: Scorpio, the Sealed Letter, beneficiary "the one who knows
   what happened at the millpond in '87." Silas: "That's me." The desk:
   "That cannot be measured." The letter is his by the will's own words and
   the house cannot release it — not refused, UNMEASURABLE. This is the
   climax: the thing he came for, in reach, kept by the second law.

3. **THE NOTICES (Ivers) — ANTICLIMAX.**
   The side door, suddenly small: if the letter can't move, change what's
   printed — the obituary names a kinder recipient. SOURCE_CONTRADICTS.
   Refused in one flat breath. After the letter, this defeat is almost
   clerical, and that smallness is the point.

4. **THE ARCHIVE (Beacon) — the fall.**
   Desperation: by candlelight he writes the millpond account into a
   receipt's margin — a confession that is mechanically a forgery: the
   claim he cannot get measured, forced into the record by hand. (v5
   scrutiny reconciliation, 2026-11-03: confession and forgery are the
   same act here; the chain breaks identically.) The chain replays. CHAIN FAILED AT
   RECEIPT INDEX 1. The house now holds a record of what he tried.

5. **THE DOOR — THE LETDOWN (ENDING RULED by David 2026-11-03 21:37:
   UNRESOLVED; REFINED by David 2026-11-03 21:58 — not black and white).**
   The binary question (does he leave or stay?) is NOT the ambiguity.
   The ambiguity is the CHARGE: Silas does not know what the house is
   holding him on — the letter claim, or the millpond itself. His mirror
   is POE, who is still in the back room not because of any will but
   because of the Griswold scandal: a printed lie (expelled from the
   University of Virginia) that no desk can measure — in the real
   software the claim is literally beyond the schema, no education key —
   so Poe's case can never close. A claim that can neither be confirmed
   nor refuted keeps its man forever. And GRISWOLD is there too, forever
   unmeasured (David, 2026-11-03 21:59): the man who printed the claims
   is kept by the same law as the man they were printed about — his
   appointment is unsupported, his memoir unmeasured; "the claims
   remain, so does he." The house does not distinguish the lie's author
   from its subject. Silas's millpond knowledge is the
   same shape. Final device: the night's last receipt is a NEW INTAKE —
   "Vane, Silas. Claim —" and the film cuts before the claim is named.
   We never learn what he is in for. (Earlier devices — disposition

## Addendum — The Threshold: why this mortuary, why machines (David, 2026-11-07)

This mortuary is not a general funeral home. It is the house cases are
REFERRED to when an instrument crosses a threshold ordinary probate
cannot settle — the hard-file house. A case is referred when the papers
contain at least one of:
  T1  beneficiaries named by DESCRIPTION or riddle, not legal identity;
  T2  COMPETING instruments (two wills, a supersession dispute);
  T3  a suspected FORGERY in the instruments;
  T4  a load-bearing claim that is UNMEASURABLE (a man's knowledge, an
      unrecorded cause, a printed claim no source can settle).
Machines handle these cases because machines refuse BY NAME and never
bend — no heir, lawyer, or celebrity can lean on them — and because a
case that cannot be closed is KEPT here, in the back room, forever.
The drawers are the precedent library: every kept case is a category
Silas's case can be compared to, and he knows it.

The known files (real cases, already measured by both desks):
- MONROE (1962): residue splits and contested readings; measured clean.
- FRANKLIN (2018): T2 — two handwritten wills; a human jury took five
  years; the desk names the governing will in one measurement (2014).
- HUGHES (1976): T3 + intestacy — no valid will; the "Mormon Will" a
  forgery. The desk cannot open the case; as a mandate it hard-tampers.
- POE (1849): a NOTICES case (T4) — Griswold's printed claims; the
  famous one (expelled from his university) is unmeasurable in the
  house's records, so the case can never close. The founding kept case.
  Griswold's own file is kept beside it.
- VANE (2026): T1 + T2 + T4 at once — riddle beneficiaries, two wills,
  and a bequest addressed to unmeasurable knowledge. The full house.
