# Demo Film — Script & Shot List (v3: the units are Apathy)

David's rulings after viewing v1, 2026-11-03: the robots must not be
humanized — at all. They are Apathy: the audit software. No "I", no
self-description, no testimony. A unit speaks only of **this desk** and
what the desk did: flat, procedural, indifferent to itself. The human
bookends (the Night Clerk) are unchanged — people may speak as people;
machines may not.

Also fixed in v3: the house clip's lab-coated man (a second, wrong human)
is cut — the only human on screen is the Night Clerk. Screen inserts are
no longer pans: each is a held framing on the decisive region (verdict,
receipt, stamp), large enough to read.

Target runtime ≈ **2:24**. Casting unchanged (Sarah = Register Unit,
Bill = Estate Unit, Alice = Notices Unit, George/clone = Night Clerk).

> "This desk seals the records. The death record. The family papers.
> The will. No claim in this house is measured against a record this
> desk has not sealed. A broken seal halts the house. Nothing
> proceeds."
