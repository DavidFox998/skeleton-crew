# SHOOTING SCRIPT v5 — "The House Keeps What It Cannot Measure"

Live form. One actor (David, as Silas Vane). The desks are voices and
work, never faces. Target 2:58. Dialogue is asymmetric by design: Silas
speaks like a man; the desks answer like procedure.

Desk voices are recorded in post (Register Unit — Sarah register; Estate
Unit — Bill register; Notices Unit — Alice register). On set, Silas plays
to counted pauses where the desk replies land. Narrator: George.

## COLD OPEN — THE DOOR (exterior, rain)

*Silas at the mortuary door, lantern raised. No dialogue. First musical
phrase sounds the governing vector: 111010010011.*

NARRATOR: Elias Vane died on All Hallows' Eve. He left two wills, a
sealed letter, and a house that measures.

TITLE: *Nothing leaves this house unmeasured.*

## BEAT 1 — THE REGISTER

*A desk. A ledger. Sealing wax, a stamp. Silas sets down his coat.*

SILAS: Silas Vane. Brother of the deceased.

REGISTER: Family record. Vane, Elias. Brother — Vane, Silas. Measured.
You may proceed.

SILAS: He sent for me, you know. Near the end.

REGISTER: That claim appears in no sealed record. It cannot be measured.

SILAS: *(a small laugh)* He was my brother. He said things.

REGISTER: What cannot be measured does not leave this house. It is kept.

SILAS: Then keep it. It's nothing.

*He goes in. The stamp comes down on his intake line. Receipt prints.*

## BEAT 2 — THE ESTATE DESK

*The two wills. Silas slides the older one across, almost gentle.*

SILAS: His will. The earlier one. The fair one.

ESTATE: Distribution under the twenty-nineteen will. Refused. Will
superseded. A later seal governs.

SILAS: He was out of his head by twenty twenty-four. Everybody knows
that.

*The desk does not answer him. The governing will is opened. Item by
item, the desk reads; after each, a receipt prints. The rhythm is the
scene's music.*

ESTATE: Taurus, the Stone Bull. Marta, the neighbor. Measured. Leo, the
Brass Telescope. The child who mapped the lion's heart. Measured.
Capricorn, the Goat Bell. The town itself. Measured.

SILAS: *(quietly, hope rising)* Three for three.

ESTATE: Scorpio. The Sealed Letter. Beneficiary — the one who knows
what happened at the millpond, in eighty-seven.

*A pause. The letter is set on the desk between them.*

SILAS: That's me. I'm the one who knows.

ESTATE: This desk cannot measure what a man knows.

SILAS: He wrote it that way so it could only be me.

ESTATE: The claim is recorded. The claim is unmeasured. The letter
remains with this house.

SILAS: The bull, the bell — fine. I came for the letter.

ESTATE: Three shares measured. One claim unmeasured. What cannot be
measured is kept.

*The printer stops. The letter stays where it was set down.*

## BEAT 3 — THE NOTICES DESK (anticlimax)

*Smaller. A press, a galley proof. Silas almost casual — too casual.*

SILAS: The obituary. Print that the letter went to his brother. It's
kinder. It's nearly true.

NOTICES: Assertion — recipient altered. The source contradicts. Refused.

SILAS: One line. Nobody checks a graveyard.

NOTICES: All entries are checked. The record is not amended.

*He looks at the proof a moment. Sets it down himself.*

## BEAT 4 — THE ARCHIVE (the fall)

*Dark. Drawers. Candlelight. No dialogue. Silas alone with a receipt
and a pen. Not altering a figure — writing, in the margin of a receipt,
what happened at the millpond. Trying to get what he knows into the
record. Any record. The house is silent for a long time.*

*Then, from everywhere and nowhere, the chain begins to replay —
receipts re-stamping themselves in the dark, one by one — and stops.*

REGISTER: The chain replays. Chain failed at receipt index one.

SILAS: I only wrote it down.

REGISTER: The attempt is recorded.

## BEAT 4B — THE BACK ROOM (the mirror)

*On the way out he passes the drawers. Two are older than the house's
paper. He stops at the first.*

SILAS: Poe. Eighteen forty-nine. He's still here?

REGISTER: The printed claims do not measure. The case does not close.

SILAS: What did he do?

REGISTER: No charge on file. One claim on file.

*The next drawer. GRISWOLD, R. W.*

SILAS: And him? He wrote the claims.

REGISTER: The appointment is unsupported. The memoir is unmeasured.
The claims remain. The subject remains.

*A long look at the two drawers. He understands his own shape.*

SILAS: Does he know?

REGISTER: This desk does not measure the kept.

SILAS: And if the claim is about the millpond —

REGISTER: This desk has no record of a millpond.

## BEAT 5 — THE DOOR (the letdown; unresolved)

*Dawn grey at the door. His coat. The lantern, unlit now.*

SILAS: Am I free to go?

*No answer. Instead, at the Register behind him, a new intake begins to
print. Line by line. VANE, SILAS. And then — CLAIM: — and the film cuts
before the claim is named. We never learn what he is in for.*

NARRATOR: The dead leave instructions. The living leave claims.

*Black.*
