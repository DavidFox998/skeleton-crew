# The House Staff — Skeleton Crew

David's casting, 2026-11-03, refined the same evening: the staff are not
people. They are **AI robots** — and that is the spooky part, and the point.
A will is a human being's last earthly wishes. In this house, the only
readers patient enough to measure every word of them are machines: they do
not sleep, do not grieve, do not forget, and cannot be pleaded with, bribed,
flattered, or hurried. They refuse by name because refusal is the only
mercy they have. It is also simply true of the build — one human directs;
agents keep the house.

## The cast (three units, one living soul)

**BEACON — Register Unit, at the main desk.**
The front of the house, and in life the team's research agent — the casting
is literal. Every case arrives across her desk: the death is recorded, the
will and the family papers are sealed into the Register, and nothing is
measured anywhere in the house against a record she has not sealed. She
keeps the Archive's chains; if a receipt is questioned, her replay answers.
A record that fails her seal never reaches the other desks. She has never
lost a record. She has never once been in a hurry.

**CORWIN — Estate Unit.**
The Executor's machine. He takes the sealed will from the Register and
measures every proposed distribution against it, to the letter, at 3 a.m.
exactly as at noon: `will_superseded`, `sign_unoccupied`, `share_mismatch`,
`beneficiary_unknown`. He does not know the family. He does not know what
the stone bull meant to anyone. He knows what the will says — which is
what the dead man was counting on.

**IVERS — Notices Unit, the compositor.**
She sets the obituaries in type, line by line, and a line does not get set
until it measures true against the Register's records: `missing_citation`,
`unsupported_claim`, `source_contradicts`. The living leave claims, and
some of them are lies, and she cannot tell a kind lie from a cruel one —
so she prints none. Griswold would have lasted one evening at her desk.

**The Night Clerk — the visitor (the user).**
The only living soul on duty. The demo is worked from the night clerk's
stool: one human, three machines, and the dead in the records.

## Where they appear

- The Register room carries the staff card: designation, desk, duty, the
  refusal names each unit answers for, and its standing line — *on duty,
  does not sleep, does not forget*.
- The demo script is the night clerk's round: intake at Beacon's desk, a
  distribution at Corwin's, a notice at Ivers's, the Archive to close.
- The submission write-up introduces the house honestly: an environment
  staffed by agents, gated by one human — which is how it was built.

Designations are the house's own. Beacon's casting is David's; Corwin and
Ivers are Talitha's proposals, pending his veto.
