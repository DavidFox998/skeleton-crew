Article II — The Six Bequests
1. ARIES — The Iron Key. To whoever first knocks thrice on the chapel door after midnight on All Hallows' Eve, I leave the iron key to the north cellar. It opens something. I no longer remember what.
2. TAURUS — The Stone Bull. To my neighbor Marta, who left bread at the gate for eleven winters and never asked my name, I leave the stone bull from my garden. Bury it with the third oak's roots, or don't. It watches either way.
3. LEO — The Brass Telescope. To the child who mapped the lion's heart from the hilltop in the summer of the comet, I leave my brass telescope. Point it at Regulus on the anniversary of my death and do not look away first.
4. CAPRICORN — The Goat Bell. To the town itself, I leave the goat bell from my door. Ring it when the fog comes off the moor wrong — you'll know what I mean — and count the echoes. If there are seven, bar the doors.
5. AQUARIUS — The Water Jar. To the well-keeper's daughter, I leave a jar of water drawn from the spring on the night the stars fell sideways. Drink it, or pour it back. Either way, remember I chose you.
6. PISCES — The Two Fishhooks. To my brother Silas, if he still walks — and he does, though not among you — I leave two fishhooks tied with silver wire. One for the river. One for the other river.
