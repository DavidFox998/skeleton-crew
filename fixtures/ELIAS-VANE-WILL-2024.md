LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF ELIAS VANE, HERMIT OF HOLLOW HILL

Being of sound and wandering mind, I leave this world as I lived in it — by the signs.

Article I — The Seal of Seven
My chart held ten wanderers in seven houses. Let the vector 111010010011 be recorded here, popcount seven, and let seven bequests issue — one for each occupied sign. No more, no fewer. Should any hand add an eighth, the gate shall refuse it.

Article II — The Seven Bequests
1. ARIES — The Iron Key. To whoever first knocks thrice on the chapel door after midnight on All Hallows' Eve, I leave the iron key to the north cellar. It opens something. I no longer remember what.
2. TAURUS — The Stone Bull. To my neighbor Marta, who left bread at the gate for eleven winters and never asked my name, I leave the stone bull from my garden. Bury it with the third oak's roots, or don't. It watches either way.
3. LEO — The Brass Telescope. To the child who mapped the lion's heart from the hilltop in the summer of the comet, I leave my brass telescope. Point it at Regulus on the anniversary of my death and do not look away first.
4. SCORPIO — The Sealed Letter. To the one who knows what happened at the millpond in '87, I leave a letter sealed in black wax. Burn it unread, or read it and burn everything else. Your choice. Both are correct.
5. CAPRICORN — The Goat Bell. To the town itself, I leave the goat bell from my door. Ring it when the fog comes off the moor wrong — you'll know what I mean — and count the echoes. If there are seven, bar the doors.
6. AQUARIUS — The Water Jar. To the well-keeper's daughter, I leave a jar of water drawn from the spring on the night the stars fell sideways. Drink it, or pour it back. Either way, remember I chose you.
7. PISCES — The Two Fishhooks. To my brother Silas, if he still walks — and he does, though not among you — I leave two fishhooks tied with silver wire. One for the river. One for the other river.

Article III — The Empty Five
Five signs stood empty: Gemini, Cancer, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius. To these I leave nothing, and that nothing is the largest bequest — let the empty rooms of the house stay empty, their doors nailed, their windows washed once a year by whoever draws the short straw.

Article IV — The Hermit's Condition
This will is sealed this 31st of October. If a later seal appears bearing my name, let the later govern — the dead may change their minds; it's the living who must keep count.

— Elias Vane, his mark X, witnessed by the moon
